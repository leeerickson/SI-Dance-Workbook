              Streaming by StreamingIdiot

              Send the show live, so family who can't be there can watch: the sound everyone hears, with the video when a number has one and a title card with the dance name the rest of the time.

              1. Open YouTube Studio and click Create → Go live. The first time, YouTube can take up to 24 hours to switch on live streaming for a channel, so do this the day before.
              2. Choose Stream. Give it a title such as “Spring Recital 2027” and choose who can watch (Unlisted means only people with the link).
              3. Copy the Stream key and paste it below.
              4. Click Start streaming here. In 10–20 seconds YouTube shows the picture; click Go live in YouTube Studio (or turn on Auto-start there so it starts by itself).
              5. Share the YouTube link with families.

              Copy the stream address and stream key from your StreamingIdiot account.

              Streaming uses some of the computer's power, at low priority so the show always comes first. Start it a few minutes early and check it on a phone. If the internet drops, it reconnects by itself.

              Test & calibrate

              Speaker test

              Walk the room: Left should come only from the left speakers, Right only from the right. "Polarity check" should sound thinner and more spread than "Both".

              Song loudness check

              Plays 20 seconds from the loudest part of the selected song at show level, through the limiter.

              Level the PA with a microphone

              Put a measurement microphone (or a laptop mic) where the audience sits. The app plays pink noise at show level and listens. If you have a sound level meter, type its reading once to calibrate the mic.
